# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective first-time and second-income investors who want a share of a residential building without buying the whole asset. They arrive cautious, comparing, and on a phone as often as a laptop. Secondary readers are people sending the site to a partner before they commit.

## Product Purpose

SERENE INVESTORS is a fictional marketing site for a fractional real estate study. It shows how a visitor browses buildings, understands the hold cycle, and reaches a sign-up decision. Success is a clear path from the first screen to Get started, with every figure labeled as sample data.

## Positioning

A measured share of a named building, shown like a catalogue, not a yield promise.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Get started. Secondary CTA: Browse properties.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Own a measured share of a building.
- Belief ladder: the product is understandable; the listings are comparable; rent and exit are explained in plain words; risk is visible; starting is small.
- Proof on hand: invented sample listings, sample stats, and sample stories in `src/data`. No real testimonials, licences, or partner marks yet.

## Brand Personality

Architectural, editorial, quiet, premium, natural. The voice is calm and precise. Emotion: confidence without heat.

## Anti-references

Stake branding, logo, copy, yields, and property names. Casino or betting sites named Stake. Generic fintech neon-green. Three equal icon cards. Invented regulators presented as fact. Dark SaaS and crypto chrome.

## Design Principles

Show the building first. Keep numbers sample-sized and labeled. Repeat one CTA intent. Leave room for documents and risk. Motion should explain sequence, not decorate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as a floor, AAA for body copy where contrast allows. Honor reduced motion by disabling Lenis and scroll-triggered motion. Do not rely on green or red alone for status.
